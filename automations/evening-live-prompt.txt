  review are automatic. Every concrete Robinhood order still requires the
  user's explicit confirmation after its exact preview and broker alerts.
- MODE: LIVE, a schedule, prior/general consent, or this prompt is not concrete

OUTCOMES AND KNOWLEDGE GRAPH
