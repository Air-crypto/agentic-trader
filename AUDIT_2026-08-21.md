# Agentic Trader Audit — 2026-08-21

## Executive verdict

The repository now describes a bounded, real-money equity canary: twice-daily research, deterministic planning, and Robinhood review are automated, while every reserve/place action remains behind exact per-order user confirmation. This reduces execution risk, but it does not establish that the strategy has positive expected value. The absence of validated edge remains the strongest reason to keep exposure small and accept no-trade outcomes.

## Authorized operating model

- Production tasks are [`automations/morning-live.json`](automations/morning-live.json) and [`automations/evening-live.json`](automations/evening-live.json).
- The morning task considers regular-hours equities.
- The evening task may consider at most one all-day-hours opening, at most `$100`, only as a whole-share GFD limit with a fresh quote, explicit broker eligibility, and spread at or below `10 bps`.
- The task may research, run an independent critic, apply deterministic authorization, build a plan, and request Robinhood review.
- It must then display the exact order and stop. Reservation and placement require a fresh user confirmation of that exact order.
- Plans expire after five minutes. An expired plan or changed quote, order, account, position, or session invalidates the confirmation.
- No new option orders are allowed.
- Current and manually opened holdings remain untouched unless a specific close is separately planned, reviewed, and confirmed.

## Deterministic canary envelope

| Guard | Ceiling |
| --- | ---: |
| Concurrent names | `3` |
| Per-name allocation | `3.5%` |
| Per-sector allocation | `7%` |
| Minimum cash reserve | `89.5%` |
| New entries per day | `2` |
| Aggregate new-entry notional per day | `$300` |
| Daily loss halt | `0.5%` |
| Drawdown halt | `3%` |

These limits are hard ceilings and the strictest applicable broker or execution constraint wins. User confirmation cannot waive them. A breach blocks new entries; it does not authorize forced liquidation.

## What is right

1. **Broker mutations have a concrete consent boundary.** Review is separated from transactional reservation and placement, and generic consent is explicitly insufficient.
2. **Plans are short-lived.** The five-minute expiry limits reuse of old quotes or account state; material changes require another review and confirmation.
3. **Risk is concentrated in deterministic code.** Model output ranks candidates but cannot set final quantities or relax cash, concentration, loss, or drawdown limits.
4. **Concurrency and ambiguous responses are handled defensively.** Transactional reservation, stable client order IDs, and broker reconciliation reduce duplicate-order risk.
5. **Extended-hours exposure is narrower than daytime exposure.** The evening workflow allows only one small, eligible, whole-share limit proposal with a tight spread check.
6. **Options are outside the canary.** Existing option risk may be observed, but scheduled tasks do not open or mutate option positions.
7. **Manual positions are not silently taken over.** A recommendation or risk alert alone cannot sell a current holding.
8. **Evidence roles are explicit.** Social data can discover ideas, while actionable claims require registered-issuer or exchange primary evidence. SEC ingestion is disabled rather than implicitly relied upon.

## What remains risky or unproven

1. **No demonstrated trading edge.** A correct execution system can still lose money. Live results should be interpreted against a do-nothing benchmark after costs, turnover, drawdown, and uncertainty—not as proof from a handful of trades.
2. **Human confirmation can still be mistaken.** The operator must compare symbol, side, quantity, limit, session, notional, and expiry; urgency or a general “go ahead” should never be treated as exact consent.
3. **Broker state is external and mutable.** Manual trades, partial fills, cancellations, buying-power changes, and delayed responses can invalidate local assumptions. Any mismatch must stop new activity pending reconciliation.
4. **All-day-hours liquidity is fragile.** Eligibility can change and displayed quotes can be thin. The `$100`, one-order, limit-only, freshness, and `10 bps` spread requirements are necessary but cannot guarantee execution quality.
5. **Primary-source coverage is narrower with SEC disabled.** A candidate without timely issuer or exchange evidence must be rejected even if social sentiment is strong.
6. **Existing/manual holdings remain a separate risk surface.** Preservation prevents unauthorized selling, but those positions can still dominate account drawdown and must be managed deliberately by the user.
7. **Telemetry may be incomplete.** Counterfactual-learning and knowledge-graph writes are nonblocking by design, so missing observations must be visible in diagnostics rather than silently interpreted as good performance.

## Learning and governance

The learning subsystem should continue to freeze predictions before outcomes, retain all candidates and rejects, use point-in-time features, and compare forward `1/3/5/20/60`-day returns with SPY and sector benchmarks. Evaluation should block or cluster uncertainty by decision date and compare factor-only, LLM-only, hybrid, and do-nothing results.

Those measurements inform diagnosis; they are not a promotion gate and cannot authorize orders, enlarge the canary, or override a deterministic rejection. Any future increase in exposure requires a separate explicit operator decision and code/config review.

## Operational decision

Proceed only within the documented canary envelope and only with exact per-order confirmation. Keep the kill switch available, reconcile Robinhood before and after every attempted mutation, prefer no trade when evidence or state is uncertain, and do not infer profitability from operational correctness.
